Questions

FAQs

  • We work on the floor in Kenya. Our clients here include Spice World Limited and Dentex Industries Limited, and we have run engagements across East Africa and the wider African market. Implementation is on-site with your team: the assessment, the rollout and the training all happen in your warehouse, not over email.

  • An ERP records what the warehouse reports. A WMS controls what the warehouse does. The gap between the two is where accuracy is lost: stock that exists on the system but cannot be found, picking confirmed before it happened, dispatch documented after the truck has left. Our WMS sits underneath your ERP, feeds it accurate movements, and shows you every message that passed between them.

  • The assessment is a 2 to 4 day on-site study. From there, scope is defined from your data rather than a template, and a signed performance baseline is agreed before anything is built. Most warehouse engagements show measurable results within four to six months, with the solution validated on your floor before full rollout.

  • That is the design constraint, not an afterthought. Screens follow the physical task, scanning replaces typing wherever possible, and training happens at the Gemba: in real conditions, under real pressure, with your own supervisors leading it. If the floor cannot run it without us standing there, it is not finished.

  • We build the review rhythms and audit structures into your team before we step back, and hand over ownership deliberately. We do not exit on a deadline. We exit when the results hold without us.
